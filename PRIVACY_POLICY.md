## 3. Information We Collect

The Bot collects different categories of information depending on which features you use. Not every user will have every category of information collected about them; collection depends on the specific commands and features you interact with.

### 3.1 Information Provided During Verification

If you use the verification system (`/verify`, `/confirmotp`, `/remindverify`), we collect:

- **Name**
- **Email address** (must be an official `@pcampus.edu.np` address), used to confirm your affiliation with Pulchowk Campus and to send a one-time password (OTP) and related notification emails
- **Date of birth**

This information is used specifically to confirm your identity as a member of the Pulchowk Campus community and, upon successful verification, to grant you the configured verified/Pulchowkian Discord role.

### 3.2 Discord Information

Because the Bot operates through Discord, we receive certain information made available to bots by Discord, including:

- Your Discord user ID, username, and display information
- Your server (guild) membership and assigned roles
- Messages, reactions, button clicks, and modal submissions where necessary for a specific bot feature to function (e.g., processing a command, moderating content, or tracking XP)
- Voice activity information (such as time spent in voice channels), where used for the leveling/statistics system

We do not claim to collect information beyond what Discord's API makes available to the Bot for its configured functionality.

### 3.3 Club and Event Information

If you register, join, or manage a club, or create or register for an event, we may collect:

- Club name, description, and membership/leadership information
- Club moderator and trusted-member assignments
- Club announcements and audit records
- Event details you submit (including event posters)
- Event registration and participation records
- Event visibility settings you choose (public, Pulchowkian-only, or private/club-only)

### 3.4 Payment Proof and Payment Information

For paid events, if you submit payment proof or payment-related information as part of event registration, this is collected and stored for the purpose of payment verification by authorized club moderators. **We do not collect or process your banking or card credentials**, and the Bot is not a payment processor. Any actual monetary transaction occurs outside the Bot through whatever method the event organizer specifies.

### 3.5 Activity and Usage Information

Depending on which features you use, we may record:

- Messages sent and voice activity, for XP/leveling and statistics purposes
- Reputation points awarded to you (`/repu`)
- Warnings and other moderation actions taken against you
- Suggestions you submit
- Birthday information, if you voluntarily set one with `/setbirthday`
- Reaction-role selections
- Other records generated by your use of bot commands

### 3.6 User-Generated Content

Content you submit through the Bot, such as suggestions, FAQ entries, club/event announcements, event posters, or payment proofs, is collected and, in some cases (e.g., approved suggestions, public announcements, FAQs), displayed to other members of the relevant Discord server.

### 3.7 Technical and Operational Information

To operate the Bot, we may store server/guild configuration, role and channel IDs, event/club configuration, club audit records, and application logs or debugging information generated by the Bot's normal operation.

We do not currently collect IP addresses, device information, or use cookies or advertising trackers through the Bot, and do not have evidence that these are collected by the underlying project.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

- Verify your affiliation with Pulchowk Campus and manage verification-related roles
- Operate core bot functionality (commands, buttons, reactions, modals)
- Provide moderation and community-safety features (warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, anti-spam)
- Administer clubs and events, including payment verification and participant exports for authorized club moderators
- Provide community engagement features such as leaderboards, reputation, suggestions, FAQs, and birthday announcements
- Send verification OTPs and related email notifications via the Gmail API
- Retrieve holiday information via the Google Calendar API and post it to configured channels
- Scrape and repost official Pulchowk Campus notices to configured channels
- Maintain the security, integrity, and proper functioning of the Bot and the community it serves

## 5. How Information Is Shared

- **Server administrators and moderators.** Authorized administrators and moderators of the relevant Discord server may have access to certain information where necessary to operate, verify, moderate, or administer the community. For example, the `/gotverified` command allows admins/moderators to view verified users' names and college email addresses, and club moderators may export event participant information (including payment status and proofs) for legitimate administration purposes. Access to these features is restricted to authorized roles.
- **Discord.** As the underlying platform, Discord has access to all data transmitted through it, subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy.
- **Third-party service providers.** Google (via the Gmail API and Google Calendar API) processes limited information as necessary to send verification/notification emails and retrieve holiday data. We do not share every category of information we collect with Google, only what is necessary for these specific functions (e.g., your email address for OTP delivery).
- **We do not sell your personal information.**
- We do not share your information with third parties for advertising purposes.
- We may disclose information where required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of FSU, the Bot's users, or others.

## 7. Data Storage

Information collected by the Bot is stored in a SQLite database (`bot.db`) used to support verification, moderation, community features, clubs, events, statistics, suggestions, and configuration. Depending on deployment, the Bot may run locally, via Docker, or on a hosting platform such as Render. Where deployed on infrastructure with ephemeral (non-persistent) storage, the database must be configured for persistent storage to avoid data loss; this is an operational/technical consideration rather than a guarantee about data retention.

## 8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including operating bot functionality, moderation, dispute resolution, security, and legitimate operational needs. **We have not defined fixed retention periods for each data category**, and the operator should establish and document specific retention schedules appropriate to each category of data (verification data, moderation records, event/payment data, etc.) prior to publishing this Policy in final form.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures intended to protect information processed by the Bot, including:

- Running the Bot's Docker image under a non-root user
- Storing secrets and credentials (such as API keys and service account credentials) via environment variables or deployment-platform secret management, rather than in source control

**We do not claim that the Bot is encrypted at rest, independently audited, penetration-tested, or certified under any specific security or compliance standard (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001), unless and until such measures are actually implemented and verified.** No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

## 10. Your Choices and Rights

- You may voluntarily add or remove certain information, such as your birthday (`/setbirthday`, `/removebirthday`).
