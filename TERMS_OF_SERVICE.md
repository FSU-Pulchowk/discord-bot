## 4. Description of the Service

The Bot provides a range of features to the Discord server(s) on which it operates, including but not limited to:

- Email verification and onboarding for Pulchowk Campus community members
- Administrative and moderation tools (warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, anti-spam, message cleanup)
- Community engagement features (leveling/XP, statistics, leaderboards, reputation points, suggestions, FAQs, birthdays, reaction roles)
- Club registration and management
- Event creation, registration, and payment verification
- Automated notifications (campus notice scraping, holiday information, birthday and club/event announcements, verification reminders)

The exact features available to you depend on the configuration of the specific Discord server and the roles/permissions you hold within it.

## 5. Verification and Onboarding

- The Bot offers an OTP-based email verification flow (`/verify`, `/confirmotp`, `/remindverify`) that uses your Pulchowk Campus email address to confirm your affiliation with the campus community.
- As part of verification, you may be asked to provide your name, Pulchowk Campus email address, and date of birth.
- You are responsible for providing accurate information during verification. Providing false or misleading verification information may result in denial of the verified role, removal of roles already granted, or other moderation action.
- Successful verification may result in the assignment of a "verified"/"Pulchowkian" Discord role, which may unlock access to additional server features.
- Non-verified users may still be able to register for certain public events through a separate OTP-based email flow, without receiving the verified role.
